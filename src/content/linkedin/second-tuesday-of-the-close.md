The second Tuesday of the close: what it should actually produce.

For most funded tech companies, the close runs over the first ten business days of the month. By the second Tuesday, the books are roughly closed and the variance commentary is being written.

The deliverable on the second Tuesday should not be a variance memo.

It should be three things.

1. The forward capital register, updated. Which dollars are now deployed against a context that died this month. Which dollars are still defendable.

2. The next call, named. The biggest unresolved decision the company has to make this month, with the three-path model attached. The math, the names, the dates.

3. The runway truth, under each of the three paths. Not the runway slide. The runway under each next-call option.

If the second Tuesday produces those three things, the variance memo writes itself. If it doesn't, the close is doing the wrong work.
