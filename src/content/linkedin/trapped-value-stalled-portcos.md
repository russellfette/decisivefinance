Put in $7,500. We name at least $22,500 of trapped value in 14 days, or the Diagnostic is free.

That sentence carries my whole business model, so here is exactly what I am claiming.

Trapped value is capital locked inside decisions whose context has died. Three tests, all three have to hold:

1. It traces to a decision. A $4K/month SaaS line is not a finding. A SaaS line bought for an initiative shelved in Q3, still auto-renewing, is.

2. It is dollarized. "We should look at cloud spend" names nothing. "$61K annualized in compute provisioned for a load profile the product no longer has" names something.

3. It comes with an accepted path. Naming money nobody will move is theater. Every line ties to a recovery move, and the CEO accepts or rejects each path on the record at Day 14.

Where it sits, in order of how often the category carries the largest finding: Cost, Pricing, Growth Spend, Product Line, Capital Structure. Pricing is usually the deepest. Cost is always the widest.

Why insiders cannot see it: the money is not hidden, it is defended. Every dollar sits behind a decision someone made in good faith, and the same traps that made the decision feel right now make reviewing it feel unnecessary.
