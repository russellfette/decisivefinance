The three-word internal memo that changes the GTM motion.

Three words. "Stop selling that."

The hardest GTM call most post-Series-A founders make in year two isn't who to hire. It's what to stop selling.

Every funded tech company has a product line, a segment, or a price tier that converts but doesn't scale. The unit economics are bad. The implementation is custom. The customer success cost eats the margin. But the revenue is real, and the team is fluent at selling it.

Killing it is the call.

The internal memo doesn't need to be long. It needs to be specific. The line item, the date the team stops quoting it, the date the team stops servicing it (different date), the comp adjustment for the reps who lose the deal flow.

Three sentences, signed by the CEO.

The longer the memo, the slower the kill. The slower the kill, the more capital leaves before the GTM motion finds its real shape.
