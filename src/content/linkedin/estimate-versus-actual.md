A busy year, a full backlog, every job priced at the margin you need. And the year still finishes a few points light.

I see this a lot in companies that bid their work. The profit gets priced in on the estimate and leaks out on the way to the invoice. Labor hours over the bid. Change orders the crew did to keep the customer happy and nobody billed. Materials bought at this quarter's price on last quarter's estimate.

Here's an illustration, not a client figure. A $500,000 job bid at 25% should leave $125,000. Run 120 extra hours, skip billing $40,000 of change orders, pay $15,000 more for materials, and it leaves about $61,600. The monthly report just shows a soft month.

The fix is boring. A one-page closeout on every finished job: estimate, actual, and why they differ. Then one change to the next bid.

And it gets the owner out of checking every big bid himself.
