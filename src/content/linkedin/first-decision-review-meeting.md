How to run the first Decision Review without blowing it up.

Three things to get right.

1. Bring three decisions to the room, not eight. The instinct is to load up. Don't. Pick the three the company can't push past this quarter. Everything else gets scheduled, not solved.

2. Bring the math, not the slide. Every decision shows up as a three-path model. Keep, Kill, Restructure. Each path has a dollar number. Each number has a defender (a name) and a date.

3. Bring the resolution, not the discussion. The point of the meeting is not to debate. It's to walk out with three resolved decisions. If a decision can't resolve in the meeting, the math wasn't ready. Send it back to the function.

What the first meeting almost always teaches you: half the decisions you thought were live are actually already made, unsigned. The other half are blocked on math that doesn't exist yet.

Both reads are useful. Both change what the second meeting looks like.

The third meeting is the one that compounds.
