    Sources & benchmark notes

    1. Burn Multiple: David Sacks, “The Burn Multiple,” Craft Ventures, 2020. Bands: <1x amazing, 1-1.5x great, 1.5-2x good, 2-3x suspect, 3x+ bad.
    2. Net Dollar Retention: Bessemer Venture Partners, State of the Cloud 2024; ICONIQ Growth, Topline Growth & Efficiency 2024. Top quartile 120%+, median ~110-115%, below 100% is net contraction.
    3. Growth rate by ARR cohort: ICONIQ Growth, Topline 2024; ChartMogul, SaaS Benchmarks Report 2024. Median YoY growth declines as ARR scales.
    4. ARR per FTE (capital efficiency): KeyBanc Capital Markets, SaaS Survey 2023 to 2024; SaaS Capital, Spending Benchmarks 2024. Public unicorn medians $300K to $500K; Series A norm $150K to $200K; Series B norm $200K to $300K.
    5. Burn / runway by cohort: ICONIQ Growth Topline 2024; Bessemer State of the Cloud 2024; informed by Decisive Finance engagements with $1M to $8M ARR Series A and B portcos. Post-2022 efficient-SaaS norms.
    6. Rule of 40: Brad Feld (popularized) and SaaS Capital methodology. Public-SaaS median fell from ~40% (2021) to ~30% (2024). Below 20% is the typical stall threshold for capital-efficient growth.
    7. Trapped value estimates: ranges reflect Diagnostic outcomes in the $1M to $8M ARR cohort. Individual results vary; the guarantee ($22,500 named on a $7,500 Diagnostic, $30,000 on $10,000) is the floor, not the median. Engagements to date: 5x to 10x.
