The seam between portfolio reporting and the next round.

Where Decisive Finance plugs into the portfolio support stack, what we install, and what flows back to the firm on a referral. Six minutes, end to end.

I.

The seam in the portfolio support stack

Stalled post-Series A/B is the segment of the portfolio your existing operator bench can't reach.

The portfolio CFO's mandate is reporting. Different output, different cadence, no overlap with forward-decision work. Growth-stage fractional CFOs won't take a stalled cap table; the math doesn't pencil for them. Platform teams have neither the mandate nor the standing capacity to defend a founder's next dollar with math, week over week, for ninety days.

The companies sitting in that seam are running the hardest forward calls of their life on instinct: pricing architecture, headcount shape, product-line direction, the path to the next round. Capital is deployed against a context that has changed. The unit economics aren't clean enough to defend a follow-on, pass a reference check from a new lead, or hold up in a board meeting. Reporting can be clean and the forward allocation still undefended.

Decisive Finance is purpose-built for that seam. Not a portfolio CFO, not a turnaround firm, not a growth-stage fractional. A different mandate, by design.
