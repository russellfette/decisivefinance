## [SECTION 1: WHY A GUARANTEE EXISTS] · width: prose (760)

### Why Decisive Finance puts the fee on the line

Most fractional CFO engagements are paid by the hour. The hour shows up. The decision does not. The risk sits with the founder.

Decisive Finance is structured as a fixed-scope, fixed-price product with a guarantee written against a measurable test. The risk sits with us. If the work does not produce the named outcome by the named date, the fee comes back, and the CEO keeps every artifact.

That structure exists for two reasons. First, the buyer is a founder making the hardest capital decisions of their career and cannot afford to add ambiguity to a finance engagement. Second, we are confident the methodology works because the same Six Trap Diagnostic™ reads the same way across every funded-tech engagement we have run.

The guarantee is not marketing. It is the mechanism that makes the work auditable.

## [SECTION 3: WHAT "EQUIVALENT MATERIALITY" MEANS] · width: prose (760)

### What "equivalent materiality" means

The 3x in-dollars floor is the default. But not every engagement surfaces dollar-recovery plans of that size. Some surface a strategic finding worth more than the dollar number, and we want the structure to recognize that without forcing the engagement into a refund just because the dollar math didn't add up.

A strategic finding of equivalent materiality is one of three things:

1. **A pricing architecture change** that the CEO accepts and commits to executing. Repricing a product line, restructuring discount discipline, or repositioning a tier with named in-market test. Worth more than the 3x dollar floor when modeled forward over 12 months.
2. **A product-line exit with board commitment.** A decision to kill, restructure, or sell a product line that the company has been carrying past the point where its context still justified it.
3. **A capital-structure change** that moves next-round valuation math. Restructuring vendor commitments, lease terms, or debt facilities in a way the next investor lead would underwrite a higher valuation against.

These are not consolation prizes. They are different shapes of the same thing: a decision the engagement made commercially defensible that was not commercially defensible before. The CEO confirms in writing. That triggers the guarantee the same way the dollar threshold does.

If neither the dollar floor nor a strategic finding lands, the fee is refunded.

## [SECTION 4: WHAT THIS GUARANTEE IS NOT] · width: prose (760)

### What this guarantee is not

To save everyone time, here is what we will not promise:

- **Cash in the bank within 30 days.** That timing depends on contracts, vendor cycles, and execution decisions the CEO controls. We promise the recovery plan is CEO-accepted and the dollar number is verifiable; we do not promise the cash-conversion timing.
- **A specific KPI movement (revenue, EBITDA, runway).** Those depend on factors outside the engagement's reach. The guarantee is on the work product, not on the company's quarter.
- **A particular acquirer interest, board reaction, or fundraising outcome.** Same reason.
- **Hours.** The engagement is a fixed-scope product. We could deliver in 60 hours or 80 hours and the guarantee runs the same way.

What we promise is the work. The decisions defended forward with math. The trapped value surfaced and recovery-pathed. The rhythm installed and in market. Those are the things we control.

## [SECTION 5: HOW TO TRIGGER THE GUARANTEE] · width: prose (760)

### How the guarantee triggers

Day 14 readout (Tier 1) and Day 90 readout (Tier 2) are scheduled at engagement letter signing. They run for 60 minutes. The CEO sees the artifacts in advance.

At each readout:

1. We walk through the artifacts and the threshold test.
2. The CEO either confirms in writing that the threshold is met, or names a specific gap.
3. If the threshold is not met and there is no equivalent-materiality finding to substitute, the refund is processed within 7 days. No back-and-forth. No internal review process. The CEO keeps every artifact.

There is no third-party arbitration baked into the structure. The CEO's written confirmation (or non-confirmation) is the trigger. We do not argue our way out of refunds.

## [SECTION 6: WHAT WE DO NOT GUARANTEE TO EVERYONE] · width: prose (760)

### Who is not a fit for this guarantee

The guarantee structure is calibrated for a specific buyer profile. It does not work for everyone.

**Not a fit if:**
- Under $1M ARR. Not enough cost structure for the dollar math to work at the 3x floor.
- Raised in the last 6 months. Forward context has not shifted enough yet for the diagnostic to pull meaningful findings.
- Already in active turnaround with another firm in the chair.
- Bookkeeping so broken the work becomes archaeology, not analysis. We will tell you on the fit call if this is the case; the cleanup is a separate fixed-fee phase, then the Diagnostic runs.
- Bootstrapped with no board, no external capital. The guarantee math works without venture backing, but the discipline this builds is calibrated for a venture-context buyer. A different operator is a better fit for bootstrapped firms.

If any of those describe your company, the fit call will surface it inside the first 10 minutes and we end clean.
