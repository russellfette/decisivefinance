## 1. Principles

1. **Buttons are 2 to 4 words.** Never 5+.
2. **Action verb first.** "Book", "Start", "Get", "See", "Take", "Install", "Run", "Claim".
3. **Every primary button has risk-reducer microcopy underneath.** 8 to 14 words.
4. **One primary CTA per section.** A secondary CTA is allowed but must be visibly softer.
5. **Dark sections use filled-orange primary + outlined-white secondary.** Light sections use filled-dark primary + outlined-dark secondary.
6. **Destination matches promise.** If the button says "Book a fit call," Calendly loads a 30-minute slot. No bait.
7. **Banned button copy:** "Contact us," "Learn more," "Submit," "Click here," "Get started," "Sign up," "Free trial."
8. **Cold readers get a fit call. Warm readers (tier cards, founding cohort) get the offer.**

## 3. Microcopy rules

The line under the button is where conversion happens.

- Lead with the time commitment if under an hour. ("30 min...")
- State one concrete takeaway. ("You leave with at least one action.")
- Never promise an outcome the call cannot deliver.
- No exclamation points. No emoji. No "we'd love to chat."
- Use "we" for the firm, "you" for the reader.

**Good:** "30 min. No pitch. You leave with at least one action."
**Bad:** "Let's chat about how we can help your company grow!"
**Bad:** "Schedule a free consultation today."
**Bad:** "Book a call to learn more about our services."

## 6. Dark vs light surface treatment

| Surface | Primary button | Secondary button | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dark (header, hero, key section, footer) | Filled orange `#e3632b`, white text | Outlined white, white text, transparent bg | The commercial moment. Generous spacing. |
| Light (interior prose, body sections) | Filled dark `#182228`, white text | Outlined dark, dark text | Softer; supports the reading flow. |

**Contrast.** Orange on dark must pass WCAG AA for text. Confirm hex values pass at 18px+ weight 400 and 14px+ weight 700.

**Button size.** 48px minimum touch target. 16 to 20px horizontal padding on desktop; 24 to 32px on mobile. Never full-width except on mobile.

**Spacing.** Microcopy sits 8 to 12px below the button, same width or slightly narrower, at 14 to 16px font size, 80 to 100% opacity of the base text color.

## 8. Banned (v2.4 sweep)

Banned button copy on this site:
- "Contact us" / "Get in touch" / "Reach out"
- "Learn more" / "Find out more"
- "Submit" / "Send"
- "Schedule a consultation"
- "Click here"
- "Download now" (use the specific thing — "Get the scorecard")
- "Sign up" (we do not have accounts)
- "Free trial" / "Try it free" (we do not have a product to try)
- "Get started" (too generic)
- "Let's chat" / "Let's talk"
- "Get fractional" (retired in v2; product name is "Decision Partnership," CTA is "Run on retainer")
- Anything with an exclamation point

Banned microcopy patterns:
- "We would love to..."
- "...our team of experts..."
- "Transform your business..."
- "Unlock [anything]" (banned word per §4)
- "Trapped capital" (retired in v2.4)
- "Forward-reallocation potential" (retired in v2.4)
- "Engine" as a noun for the system being installed (retired; use "rhythm")
